Sips

Water tracking for your wrist

Sips is a water tracker built for Apple Watch from the ground up. Log a sip with one tap and see your daily hydration progress at a glance — no phone needed.

Privacy

Sips does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All your hydration and weight data lives securely in Apple Health on your device. We have no servers, no accounts, and no analytics.

When you log water in Sips, the app writes directly to Apple Health and reads from it to show your progress. Nothing leaves your device.

We do not have access to your data, and we never will — because we never built a way to receive it.
